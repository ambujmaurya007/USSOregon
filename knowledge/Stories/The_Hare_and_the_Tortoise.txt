# The Hare and the Tortoise

**Language:** English
**Age range:** 4-8
**Topic:** patience, effort, moral story
**Aliases:** hare and tortoise, slow and steady, tortoise race
**Category:** story
**Activity type:** story, moral, bedtime
**Exact recitation:** false

Once upon a time, there lived a hare and a tortoise in a forest. The hare was very proud of his speed. Whenever he saw the tortoise, he laughed at him and said, “You are so slow! You will never win a race against me.”

The tortoise was calm and patient. He said, “Let us have a race. We shall see who wins.”

The hare agreed and the race began. The hare ran very fast and soon left the tortoise far behind. He became overconfident and thought, “I am so fast that I can rest for a while.”

So he sat under a tree and went to sleep. Meanwhile, the tortoise kept walking slowly but steadily. He never stopped and kept moving ahead.

In the meantime, the hare woke up and saw that the tortoise was very near the finishing line. He ran as fast as he could, but it was too late. The tortoise had already reached the end and won the race.

The hare was ashamed of his foolishness. He realized that speed without patience can never win.

**Moral: Slow and steady wins the race.**
