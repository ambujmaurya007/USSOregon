# Baa Baa Black Sheep

**Language:** English
**Age range:** 2-5
**Topic:** nursery rhyme, sheep, poem
**Aliases:** baa baa black sheep, black sheep poem
**Category:** poem
**Activity type:** recite, sing, rhyme
**Exact recitation:** true

Baa, baa, black sheep,
Have you any wool?
Yes, sir, yes, sir,
Three bags full.

One for the master,
One for the dame,
One for the little boy
Who lives down the lane.
