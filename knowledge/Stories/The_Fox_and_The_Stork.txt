# The Fox and the Stork

**Language:** English
**Age range:** 4-8
**Topic:** friendship, honesty, moral story
**Aliases:** fox and stork story, stork dinner, friendship lesson
**Category:** story
**Activity type:** story, moral, bedtime
**Exact recitation:** false

Once upon a time, a fox and a stork were good friends. The fox invited the stork to dinner and served soup in a flat bowl. The stork had a long beak and could not drink the soup easily.

The fox licked up the soup quickly and enjoyed his meal. The stork was still hungry and felt very disappointed. He was not able to eat even a little.

The next day, the stork invited the fox to dinner. This time, the stork served the soup in a long-necked bottle. The fox’s mouth was wide but his neck was short. He could not drink the soup at all.

The stork drank the soup happily and smiled. The fox understood that he had been fooled.

The fox realized that the stork had taught him a good lesson. He felt ashamed of his selfishness. Since then, the fox and the stork never became friends again, but both learned the importance of being fair and kind.

**Moral: Treat others the way you would like to be treated.**
