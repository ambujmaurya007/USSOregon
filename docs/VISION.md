# Baby Girl AI Project — Project Vision

## Overview
Build a **local-first AI companion that grows with my daughter over many years**.

The long-term vision is an AI companion that starts as a software/mobile application and eventually becomes embodied in a **physical Teddy Bear**.

The AI should not feel like a generic chatbot. It should develop a consistent personality, remember relevant preferences and interactions, adapt its communication to the child's age, and become a long-term companion.

## Core Priorities
- Natural voice conversation
- Personalized storytelling
- Consistent Teddy personality
- Long-term memory
- Age-appropriate interaction
- Educational assistance as the child grows
- Privacy and local processing where practical

## Product Concept

### The Interaction
Child: *"Teddy, tell me a story about an elephant."*

Teddy: *Generates a personalized story based on the child's age, preferences, previous interactions, and Teddy's consistent personality.*

### Example Personalization
- Child likes elephants
- Child previously enjoyed adventure stories
- Teddy remembers a recurring fictional character

**Result:** "Of course! Shall we continue the adventure of Ellie the Elephant?"

### Product Value Proposition
**The companion remembers and grows with the child.**

## Scope (What Is NOT a Priority Now)
These ideas have been deprioritized for MVP:
- Birthday photo archives
- Family photo search
- Large media storage
- Digital scrapbook generation
- Complex family history systems

**Focus:** The AI companion experience

## Implementation Timeline
1. **Phase 1 (MVP):** Voice pipeline with local LLM
2. **Phase 2:** Personality and memory system
3. **Phase 3:** Multiple model support and router
4. **Phase 4:** Mobile interface
5. **Phase 5:** Physical Teddy embodiment

## Long-Term Vision
The system evolves from software → mobile → physical hardware, with AI processing distributed across home servers and eventually integrated into the Teddy itself.
